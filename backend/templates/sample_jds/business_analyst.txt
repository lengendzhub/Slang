Role: Senior Business & Data Analyst
Company: FinTech Growth Dynamics
Location: Hybrid / New York, NY

About the Role:
We are looking for an analytical thinker to sit at the intersection of business strategy and technical systems. You will analyze high-volume data streams, evaluate workflow bottlenecks, and collaborate directly with product and engineering leaders to deliver measurable cost reductions and efficiency gains.

Key Responsibilities:
- Audit operational workflows and data pipelines to identify cost-saving and optimization opportunities.
- Partner with product managers and engineering teams to translate business questions into analytical specifications.
- Build executive dashboards and weekly KPI reports monitoring operational efficiency and vendor performance.
- Perform root cause analyses on data anomalies and discrepancies.

Mandatory / Essential Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in business analysis, data analytics, or technical operations.
- Advanced SQL proficiency (window functions, subqueries, query plan optimization).
- Proven track record translating complex quantitative findings into clear executive business recommendations.
- Experience with data visualization platforms (Tableau, PowerBI, or Looker).

Required Qualifications:
- Familiarity with Python or R for data exploration and reporting automation.
- Demonstrated ability collaborating across multi-disciplinary teams (engineering, product, finance).

Preferred / Nice to Have:
- Knowledge of cloud data infrastructure (PostgreSQL, Snowflake, or BigQuery).
- Experience evaluating third-party vendor SLAs and cloud operational expenditures.
