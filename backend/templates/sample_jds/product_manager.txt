Role: Technical Product Manager (Data & Developer Platforms)
Company: DataPulse Technologies
Location: Hybrid / New York, NY

About the Role:
DataPulse is looking for a Technical Product Manager to bridge the gap between engineering teams and enterprise customers. You will drive roadmap planning, feature scoping, and customer discovery for our real-time developer APIs and SQL query optimization platform.

Key Responsibilities:
- Own the product lifecycle from customer problem discovery to release, sprint planning, and telemetry tracking.
- Translate complex distributed systems capabilities and API designs into customer-facing PRDs.
- Partner closely with engineering leads on architectural tradeoffs and delivery milestones.
- Act as the voice of technical users and developer communities.

Mandatory / Essential Requirements:
- 3+ years experience in technical product management or a background as a software engineer transitioning into product.
- Strong technical fluency: ability to read code, understand database schemas, and discuss API contracts with staff engineers.
- Proven ability to define clear PRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria.

Transferable / Adjacent Experience:
- Engineering mentorship, cross-functional project leadership, or technical community stewardship.
- Building developer tools, open-source projects, or developer documentation.

Preferred:
- Experience with SQL optimization tools, distributed caching, or cloud infrastructure.
