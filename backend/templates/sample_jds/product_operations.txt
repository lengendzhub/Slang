Role: Product Operations Manager
Company: ScaleVelocity Platforms
Location: Hybrid / San Francisco, CA

About the Role:
We are hiring our first Product Operations Manager to supercharge our product development engine. You will optimize the feedback loop between customers, sales, engineering, and product managers, ensuring that high-priority user feedback directly informs our roadmap and releases execute with zero friction.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and manage scalable operational processes for collecting, prioritizing, and analyzing product feedback across channels.
- Partner with engineering and product squads to monitor launch health, feature flag rollouts, and regression triage.
- Build internal dashboards tracking release velocity, customer dispute frequencies, and platform reliability.
- Establish standard operating procedures (SOPs) and train cross-functional teams on new product capabilities.

Mandatory / Essential Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in product operations, technical program management, or strategy operations at a technology company.
- Exceptional analytical and data interpretation skills (SQL and spreadsheet modeling required).
- Experience partnering closely with product managers and software engineering leads.
- Proven ability to design workflows from scratch and streamline chaotic operational bottlenecks.

Required Qualifications:
- Familiarity with modern product management tools (JIRA, Linear, Notion, LaunchDarkly).
- Strong track record managing cross-functional projects with tight deadlines.

Preferred / Nice to Have:
- Experience in B2B SaaS or developer-facing products.
- Basic understanding of API integrations and webhook telemetry.
